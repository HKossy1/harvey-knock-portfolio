Natural Design

Solo project · Photography, writing & editorial design · Perfect bound, printed by Mixam

A photography led publication exploring the idea that natural beauty is not a matter of aesthetics, but of function made visible. Each chapter looks at a different visual system - symmetry, fractals, branching, flow, texture - to understand not just why these forms arise, but why we're so drawn to them. It moves between biology and psychology, between the mechanisms of life and the ways we perceive them.
