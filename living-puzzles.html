Living Puzzles

Solo project · Object design · Laser-cut acrylic, printed vinyl

A physical continuation of the Coded Visual systems, translating the same grid and rotation logic into abstract puzzles. With no correct solution, Living Grid and Living Cycle encourage you to discover your own composition with each rearrangement creating a new, bespoke structure of pattern, balance and growth.
