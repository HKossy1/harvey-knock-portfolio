About

My work returns to the idea that pattern is not just decorative but a way of understanding how we relate to the world. Natural structures like symmetry and growth carry meaning worth paying attention to, not just admiring.

I'm drawn to making that engagement active rather than passive, using photography, code and interaction to invite people to notice, participate in, and reconnect with the patterns around them.

I graduated from Falmouth University with First Class Honours in Graphic Design, and I'm based in Somerset. I'm looking for work at the crossroads of nature, science and design.
